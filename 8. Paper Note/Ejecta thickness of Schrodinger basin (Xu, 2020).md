# **2. Method**

d0-Δd=T_inner

# **3. Analyses and Results**

### 3.1 For T_ED

T_inner/T_outer = 3

### 3.2 From T_ED to T_PE

with the consideration of shielding effect (本地物质阻止了新的ejecta击穿到crust)

Adopt the exponent index -3 (not have father crater)

Adopt the revised ballistic sedimentation model Xie (2020)

计算出 T_LM 中位值并拿求得的T_ED相减得到T_PE

### 3.3 Transient crater

已知T_PE可以通过公式计算Rt，知道了transient crater depth就可以知道Schrodinger是否击穿了crust，按此论文方法，**未击穿**

### 3.4 Rim Uplift

已知T_PE，已知高程差，相减可得到uplift的值，结果显示uplift占主导，占到76%
